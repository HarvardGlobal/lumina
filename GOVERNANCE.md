# LUMINA governance

LUMINA is maintained by HarvardGlobal – Division of Clinical Information, Beth
Israel. Maintainers review contributions for technical quality, security,
health-data governance, licence compatibility, and project fit.

At least one maintainer approval is required before merging. Changes affecting
patient-data handling, identity, standards mappings, deployment security, or
external component pins require explicit maintainer review and may require
clinical, privacy, security, or legal review outside GitHub.

Maintainers may decline features that do not fit the project scope. Project
roadmap and release decisions remain with the maintainers.
